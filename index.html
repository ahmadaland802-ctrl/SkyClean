THE IDEA

A small prototype for a big environmental question.

🎈

Why a balloon?

The helium balloon provides lift so a lightweight purification pod can be demonstrated above a miniature city environment.

🌿

Why filtration?

The prototype explores moving air through particulate filtration and activated carbon, then comparing the air before and after treatment.

Prototype, not a city-scale purifier. SkyClean is an exhibition demonstration designed to test the concept safely at small scale.

HOW IT WORKS

TECHNOLOGY

Lightweight by design.

🎈

Helium balloon

Provides the lifting force for the aerial demonstration.

🌀

Mini blower

Creates the airflow through the filter stack.

⬜

HEPA media

Targets fine particulate matter in the demonstration airflow.

⚫

Activated carbon

Supports adsorption of some gases and odors.
